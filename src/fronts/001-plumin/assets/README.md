# Imagen de la caja de muestras

`sample-box.webp` es una visualización de producto ficticio creada con la herramienta integrada imagegen. Representa cinco viales de 4 ml; los colores de la foto son ilustrativos y la selección real se muestra en «Tu caja».

Se exportó a WebP de 1200 × 800 para la sección de muestras, con carga diferida.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: product photograph for the Plumín fountain pen ink sample box website. Primary request: premium realistic studio product photo of an open small cream paperboard presentation box containing exactly five miniature 4 ml clear glass ink sample vials, black screw caps, arranged neatly in a fitted paper insert. Inks are deep burgundy, forest green, cobalt blue, warm amber and violet. A simple cream lid rests slightly behind the box. Text (verbatim): "plumín" in small elegant dark navy lowercase lettering centered on the lid, and "5 × 4 ml" in very small lettering below; no other text. Scene/backdrop: warm off-white seamless tabletop, subtle paper grain. Composition: landscape 3:2, three-quarter overhead view, tight composition focused on the box with comfortable margins, entire product visible, no hands, no pen, no unrelated props. Lighting: soft daylight from upper left, realistic gentle shadows, tactile understated editorial product photography. No watermarks. This is a fictional product visualization for a demo website.
